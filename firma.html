How to set up in Outlook (Mac or Windows)?

  1. Click the **"Copy Signature to Clipboard"** button above (this will copy the formatted HTML layout enriched with absolute images).
  2. Open **Microsoft Outlook** on your computer.
  3. Go to the **Outlook** menu (on Mac) or **File** (on Windows) and select **Preferences** or **Options**, then click on **Signatures**.
  4. Click the **"+"** or **"New"** button to create a new signature, and give it a name (e.g. *Sebastián Montero - Golden Seafood*).
  5. Click on the signature edit box at the bottom and press **Cmd + V** (on Mac) or **Ctrl + V** (on Windows) to paste the signature.
  6. The layout, high-fidelity logo, golden divider, and data will appear identical to the physical card.
  7. In the "Choose default signature" section, select your new signature for "New messages" and "Replies/forwards".
  8. Done! Save the changes and your signature will be active on all your sent emails.
